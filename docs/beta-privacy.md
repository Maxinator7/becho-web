# Becho beta — privacy notice and test terms

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Becho is in a **closed beta**. You've been invited to try an early version and tell us what works and what doesn't. Please read this short notice before you sign up.

## This is a test version
- Features may change, break or disappear, and **all beta data (accounts, listings, chats) may be reset or deleted** at any time, including at the end of the beta.
- Demo listings marked "(Demo)" are examples, not real items for sale.
- Becho does not handle payments. If you choose to meet someone and exchange an item, that's between the two of you — meet in a public place and check the item before paying.
- Please **don't upload** identity documents, bank details or anything you wouldn't want a small group of testers to see.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** your name, email address and mobile number, and your password (stored only as a one-way hash).
- **What you post and do:** listings, photos and videos, chat messages, offers, saved ads and feedback you send.
- **Technical and usage data:** device and browser type, pages and screens you visit, and clicks. On the website we use PostHog analytics, which can record sessions to help us find bugs — **everything you type into forms is masked** and never recorded.

## Why we use it
To run the beta (sign-in, listings, chat and offers), to keep it safe (screening listings, stopping abuse), and to improve Becho based on how it's used and what you tell us.

## Who processes it
Our service providers store or process data on our behalf: Neon (database), Render (servers), Cloudflare (website and photo storage), Brevo (verification emails) and PostHog (analytics). We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Your choices
- You can edit your name and see your details on the Account page.
- To have your account and data deleted, send us a request through the **Feedback** button (choose "Other" and write "Delete my account"). We'll confirm when it's done.
- By creating an account you agree to these beta terms and this notice.
